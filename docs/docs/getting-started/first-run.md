# First Run

When you launch EnglishCoach Pro for the first time, you'll go through a
5-step onboarding wizard:

## Step 1 — Profile

Enter your name and pick an avatar emoji.

## Step 2 — Exam Target

Select your target exam (TOEIC, IELTS, TOEFL, VSTEP) and set your target score.
The slider range adjusts per exam type:

| Exam | Score Range |
|------|------------|
| IELTS | 0.0 – 9.0 |
| TOEIC | 10 – 990 |
| TOEFL | 0 – 120 |
| VSTEP | A1 – C1 |

## Step 3 — Exam Date

Enter your exam date. The app shows a countdown and warns if less than 30 days remain.

## Step 4 — Free Time

Set your weekly availability with morning/afternoon/evening time slots and durations.

## Step 5 — Placement Test

Take the adaptive placement test (recommended) or skip if you know your level.
